Breakfast is a calorie and macro tracker built around one typed line. A line such as "2 tbsp oats, half a tbsp peanut butter, banana" becomes portions, foods and nutrition, worked out from real food records.

The diary is kept on the phone and works offline, with no account and no cloud sync.

Features:
- Several foods in one line, with amounts in grams, millilitres, servings or words like "half a tbsp".
- Frequent and recent foods at the top of the list.
- A bundled USDA food database that needs no connection.
- Barcode scanning and Open Food Facts search for packaged products, with optional corrections sent back.
- Personal foods and recipes, with custom servings and aliases.
- A diary of Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Snacks for each day. Logged entries keep their numbers when a food changes later.
- A daily burn estimate from height, age, weight, sex and activity, adjusted over time by logged meals and weigh-ins.
- Goals to lose, maintain or gain at a chosen pace, with macro targets in percent or grams.
- A weight chart with a trend line and a projected date range for the target weight.
- Weekly macro bars against the daily goal.
- A tracking-only mode with no targets or weights.
- Macro colours for protanopia, deuteranopia or tritanopia, or monochrome.
- Export and import of the whole diary as one file.

Latin and Cyrillic food names are both supported and searched separately.

Open Food Facts is the only online service in the app. Search words or a barcode reach it only on a submitted search, "Find more matches", Add with foods that have no match, or a scan of a barcode not in the Library. Barcode lookups can be turned off in Settings, and searching as you type stays off unless turned on after a warning. Contributing to Open Food Facts is a separate, optional action that needs an Open Food Facts account, and the password is saved, encrypted, only if you choose.

The bundled food data comes from USDA FoodData Central (FNDDS 2021-2023 and SR Legacy 2018-04, CC0). Product data comes from Open Food Facts contributors under the Open Database License.
